Privacy Policy

Bridge Bounce: Jump Challenge (Android) · Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GAD Games Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Bridge Bounce: Jump Challenge (the "Game"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The Game shows ads and uses analytics. Our partners (listed below) collect certain device-level data to deliver ads and measure how the Game is used.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the Game, the third-party services described in section 4 may automatically collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers – Android Advertising ID, app instance ID, device model, operating system version, language, screen size, mobile network information.
  • Network information – IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) may be derived.
  • App activity – for example game events such as level started, level finished and character skin unlocked, plus ad impressions and ad clicks.
  • Diagnostics and performance data – technical signals about how the Game and ads perform.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages, or any other personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, unlocked characters and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game removes it.

The Game may show local reminder notifications (for example, a daily reward). They are generated on your device; you can turn them off in your Android system settings.

4. Third-party services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) from third parties. They act under their own privacy policies:

  • Appodeal – ad mediation. Appodeal and the ad networks it works with (for example Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle/Liftoff, Mintegral, ironSource, BidMachine and others) use the identifiers above to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized. See the Appodeal Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics – analytics about how the Game is used. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity – the game engine. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

We share the data described in section 2 with these providers for advertising and analytics purposes. We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money.

5. Your choices

  • Ads personalization – on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID.
  • Notifications – disable them in Android Settings → Apps → Bridge Bounce → Notifications.
  • Data rights – depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not hold data that identifies you directly, most of these requests must be made to the relevant partner (see section 4). You can also contact us using the details below.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

7. Retention and security

Data collected by third-party SDKs is retained according to their policies. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported by the respective provider.

8. International transfers

Our partners may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
